About the Flying Project

Project Overview

Our flying project is an advanced engineering challenge combining aerodynamics, materials science, and mechanical design. Team THE BAKED has developed a lightweight, efficient flying machine capable of competing in university engineering competitions. The project demonstrates our team's ability to design, build, test, and optimize complex mechanical systems.

Project Progress

From concept to completion

Phase 1: Design

Design Process

During the design phase, we explored what shape the hovercraft should have and where the fans should be placed. We made and compared several simple models, looked at different possibilities, and chose the design that seemed the most practical to build and test.

Phase 2: Building

Construction & Assembly

The building phase involved precise fabrication of all components using advanced materials and manufacturing techniques. Our team worked on creating the hull structure, installing the propulsion system, mounting the control electronics, and assembling the skirt system. Each component was carefully crafted to meet exact specifications and weight requirements.

Construction Timelapse

Phase 3: Testing

Testing & Optimization

Comprehensive testing was performed to validate all systems and optimize performance. We conducted wind tunnel tests to verify aerodynamic efficiency, track testing to measure speed and acceleration, and stability tests to ensure safe operation. Each test provided valuable data that led to further refinements and improvements to our design.

Testing Results & Analysis

Phase 4: Final Product

Finished Product & Competition

The final hovercraft represents the culmination of months of design, engineering, and testing. Our team successfully brought all components together to create a high-performance machine capable of competing at the highest level. The finished product demonstrates innovation, precision engineering, and flawless execution. We are proud to present our hovercraft and its competition results.
